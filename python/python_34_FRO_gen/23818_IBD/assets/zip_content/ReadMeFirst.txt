This package is designed for use in IBUCs with serial numbers beginning with TE80 or TE90.
It will NOT work with any other IBUCs.
Upgrading IBUCs with serial numbers not beginning with TE80 or TE90 will cause the
IBUC to become inoperable and require return to the factory to fix.
Upgrading must use the supplied upgrade utilities ArmSerialDownloader.exe and ArmUdpDownloader.exe.
ArmUdpDownloader.exe is the preferred program for upgrading.
